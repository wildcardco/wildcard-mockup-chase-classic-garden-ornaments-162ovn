The Yard

A stone yard you drive out to on purpose

At the end of Longshadow Lane in rural Pomona, statues, fountains, and urns stand out among the trees the way they will one day stand in your garden — at real scale, in real light, already weathering.

We are a small, personal operation, not a big-box nursery aisle. Every piece here is hand-selected: classical and European-style reproduction statuary, cast stone and natural stone work, and finishes that are aged and patinated rather than painted to look new. You walk the yard, circle a piece, put a hand on it, and know.

Most of our visitors come out from Carbondale, Murphysboro, and Makanda — or make a day of it alongside the Shawnee Hills. It is worth the drive, and we treat it that way.

Worth the Drive

  • Curated, not mass-market. Hand-selected classical pieces, no plastic lawn decor.
  • Built to improve with age. Stone that takes on moss, lichen, and patina by design.
  • Seen at true scale. Judge proportion in person, in a garden setting, before you commit.
  • Personal help. One small operation, from selection through delivery and placement.

The Stone

Made to be left out in the weather

Everything in the yard is chosen against one measure: will it be more beautiful in twenty years? Cast stone and natural stone are the opposite of disposable decor — frost, rain, and time are collaborators, not threats.

Many of our pieces come already weathered or finished with aged, patinated surfaces, so a new fountain or urn doesn't sit raw and white in an established garden. It arrives looking like it belongs.

Cast Stone

Dense, crisp reproduction of classical carving that develops lichen and patina like quarried stone — the traditional material of European garden ornament.

Natural Stone

Quarried material with the weight, tooling marks, and variation that no molded resin can imitate.

Aged & Patinated Finishes

Weathered limestone grays, moss-ready surfaces, and bronze-verdigris tones — finishes that give a new piece an old soul.

Plan a Visit

The yard is the showroom

There is no substitute for standing beside a fountain to judge its scale, or seeing how dusk light falls across a weathered urn. We invite you to come walk the yard — it's a beautiful drive through the Shawnee Hills from Carbondale, Murphysboro, or Makanda, and the pieces are best seen in morning or golden-hour light.

As a small rural operation, our hours follow the seasons. If you're traveling any distance, or coming with a specific piece or project in mind — a designer's plan, a courtyard, an estate garden — we're glad to have advance notice by post so we can have the right pieces accessible and time set aside for you.

Landscape designers and trade visitors are especially welcome; bring your drawings.
